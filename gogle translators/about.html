About Global Text and Document Translators

Global Text and Document Translators is a browser-based tool for people who need help understanding or preparing text in another language. It brings text entry, voice dictation, document text extraction, translation, and downloadable results together in one responsive interface.

How to use the translator

  1. Choose the language of your text and the language you want.
  2. Type in the text box or use the microphone where your browser supports speech recognition.
  3. For a document, choose File translation and upload a supported file of up to 15 MB.
  4. Review the translated result and download it as TXT, HTML, or PDF.

Supported files and limitations

The tool extracts text from selected text documents, PDFs, spreadsheets, presentations, and images. It does not convert every file type, preserve the original document layout, or guarantee that scanned pages and handwriting can be recognized. Some language pairs or browser speech features may not be available.

Our approach

We aim to make the translation workflow clear and accessible on phones and computers. Translation and speech recognition may rely on third-party services, so results and availability depend on those providers as well as your browser and internet connection.
