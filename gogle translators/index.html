A simple way to translate text and documents

      
Choose the language you are translating from and the language you need. Type or dictate text, then select Translate to see the result. For a document, choose File translation, upload a supported file, and download the translated text in your preferred format.

      
Supported file types and limits

      
The translator can extract text from common document, spreadsheet, presentation, and image formats. Files are limited to 15 MB. Scanned documents and images may need OCR, and complex layouts, tables, handwriting, or embedded graphics may not be preserved. Downloads contain translated text rather than the original document's full formatting.

      
Translation quality and privacy

      
Translations are generated by third-party translation services and may contain errors. Review important or professional translations before relying on them. Extracted text is sent to the selected translation service to produce a result; do not upload confidential or sensitive material.
