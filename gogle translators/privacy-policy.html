Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains what happens when you use Global Text and Document Translators. The site is a static browser-based application and does not provide an account or a server for storing uploaded documents.

Text and document translation

Files are opened in your browser to extract text. To produce a translation, the text you enter or extract is sent over the internet to third-party translation services used by the app, including Google translation endpoints and MyMemory as a fallback. Those providers may process or retain requests under their own privacy policies. Do not submit confidential, sensitive, or legally protected information.

Voice input

If you use the microphone, your browser asks for microphone permission and provides speech recognition when supported. Recognition may be processed by your browser provider or another service; we do not operate a voice-recognition server or intentionally store voice recordings.

Service providers and technical data

The site loads software libraries and fonts from third-party content delivery networks. Those providers may receive technical request data such as your IP address and browser information. Your browser and hosting provider may also maintain standard security logs.

Advertising and cookies

Advertising is not active on this site until the owner adds an approved AdSense account and ad code. If advertising is enabled later, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, and personalize ads as permitted by law and your consent choices. Visitors in regions requiring consent will be shown an appropriate consent flow before relevant advertising technologies are used.

Your choices and contact

You can choose not to use voice input, avoid submitting personal or sensitive text, or stop using the site. For privacy questions or requests, contact the site owner, CH M Saqib, at msaqibshafiq7252@gmail.com or via WhatsApp.

Google privacy information: policies.google.com/privacy.
